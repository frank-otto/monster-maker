# Sounds – sources & licenses (Monster Maker)

All sounds are recorded samples released under **CC0 1.0** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No synth beeps, no oscillators. `tools/build.mjs` embeds them as base64 into `js/sounds-src.js` so the game also works via `file://`. Credit is not required for CC0 and is given voluntarily, by source ID only. The stone-tower room sound is a generated convolution reverb.

## Music
Toccata and Fugue in D minor, BWV 565 (J. S. Bach) – the composition is in the public domain. The game plays our own short arrangement of the opening, plus original D-minor lab, order and showdown grooves, sequenced note by note with the samples below.
